Complete the Google Cloud Education Credit coupon process for IA340.

The workflow is:

**open the course link → request a coupon → verify your JMU email → receive the coupon → redeem it in Google Cloud → confirm your course Cloud Billing account**

## Important — use the correct Google Account

For IA340, use the **Google Account you created with your JMU email address** — your **JMU-associated Google Account**.

**Do NOT redeem the coupon into your regular personal Gmail account.**

Your JMU email is used in two parts of this process:

1. Google sends the student-verification message and coupon information to your **JMU school email**.
2. When Google Cloud asks you to sign in during redemption, use that same **JMU school email** to sign in to the **JMU-associated Google Account** you created for IA340.

If you have multiple Google accounts open in the same browser, it is easy to redeem the coupon into the wrong account. For that reason, **use a Private / Incognito browser window for the redemption step**.

---

## Step 1 — Open the Student Coupon Retrieval Link

Your instructor will send you a **Student Coupon Retrieval Link** for the IA340 Google Cloud Education Credit.

Open that URL in your browser.

---

## Step 2 — Enter Your Name and JMU Email

On the coupon request page:

1. Enter your name.
2. Enter your **JMU school email address**.
3. Submit the request.

Google uses the school email address to verify that you are an eligible student for this course.

*Example of the student coupon request page. Enter your name and JMU school email, then submit the request.*

---

## Step 3 — Verify Your JMU Email

After you submit the request, check your **JMU email inbox**.

Google will send a verification message to the JMU email address you entered.

1. Open the verification email.
2. Click the verification link.
3. Complete the email verification.

If you do not see the message, check spam/junk and make sure you entered your JMU email correctly.

---

## Step 4 — Receive Your Coupon

After your JMU email is verified, Google will send another email containing your **Google Cloud Education Credit coupon**.

The coupon email should contain:

- a **coupon code**; and
- a **Redeem now** link.

Keep this email open. You will use it to redeem the coupon.

---

## Step 5 — Redeem the Coupon in a Private / Incognito Window

<div style="background: #fff8c5; border: 1px solid #d4a72c; border-left: 5px solid #9a6700; border-radius: 6px; padding: 1rem 1.25rem; margin: 1.25rem 0;">
  <strong style="color: #7d4e00; font-size: 1.05em;">🛑 Crucial Account-Safety Step:</strong>
  <p style="margin: 0.5rem 0 0; color: #4c2800;">
    <strong>Do not redeem the coupon in your normal browser window if your personal Gmail account is already signed in there.</strong>
  </p>
</div>

### Recommended method

1. Open a **new Private / Incognito browser window**.
2. In that private window, sign in to Google using your **JMU school email address**.
3. Make sure this signs you in to the **JMU-associated Google Account** you created for IA340.
4. In the same private window, open the **Google Cloud Console** and confirm that the JMU-associated Google Account is the active account.
5. Still in the same private window, open the **Redeem now** link from the coupon email. If needed, copy the redemption URL from the email and paste it into the private window.
6. Confirm again that the JMU-associated Google Account is active before you continue.
7. If the coupon code is not already filled in, copy the coupon code from the email and paste it into the **Coupon code** field.
8. Review the information and terms.
9. Click **Accept and continue** to redeem the coupon.

### Another acceptable method

You may also open the **Redeem now** link directly in a new Private / Incognito window first. When Google asks you to sign in, enter your **JMU school email address** and sign in to the JMU-associated Google Account before you accept the coupon.

> **Never continue if your regular personal Gmail account is shown as the active Google account.**
>
> If the wrong account appears, stop, close the window, open a fresh Private / Incognito window, and sign in again with the JMU-associated Google Account.

You should not need to enter a credit card for this education-credit process. If Google asks you to start the regular Google Cloud Free Trial or enter credit-card information, stop and contact the instructor before continuing.

---

## Step 6 — Confirm Your Course Cloud Billing Account

After you select **Accept and continue**, Google adds the education credit to a **Cloud Billing account named for the course**.

You do **NOT** create this billing account manually as part of coupon redemption.

Before you finish, confirm that the course Cloud Billing account / education credit appears in your Google Cloud account. If needed, open **Billing** in the Google Cloud Console and look for the course billing account.

**Be ready to show the instructor that the course Cloud Billing account is present.**

This page covers **coupon redemption and billing-account confirmation only**. Google Cloud project setup will be handled separately in class.
