Four questions — 40 points

Q1. What are the 20 tweets with the most likes? — 10 points

Use MongoDB AI to retrieve your 20 most-liked tweets. Display their complete tweet text and like counts in a pandas table. In Markdown, briefly describe what these popular tweets discuss.

Q2. Which 10 users have the most followers? — 10 points

Create a bar chart showing usernames and follower counts for the ten most-followed users in your collection. Briefly identify the leading users in your Markdown findings.

Q3. Which profile locations are most common among your users? — 10 points

Use user.location to create a bar chart of the most common user profile locations. Base the counts on users, not their number of tweets. In Markdown, identify the leading locations and note that these are self-reported profile locations.

Q4. Which 10 hashtags are most popular? — 10 points
